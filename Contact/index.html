Frequently Asked Questions

What is Honey & Hymns?

Honey & Hymns is a faith-centered space for homemakers, homeschool moms, and women growing in their faith. You'll find encouragement, practical resources, homeschooling ideas, homemaking inspiration, and reflections on the beautiful ordinary moments of family life.

Is Honey & Hymns Catholic?

Yes. Honey & Hymns is rooted in the Catholic faith and created especially for women who want to bring their faith into everyday family life.

Do I have to be Catholic to enjoy Honey & Hymns?

Not at all! Although I’m Catholic and Honey & Hymns is rooted in the Catholic faith, women from any Christian denomination are welcome here. My hope is that Honey & Hymns can be a warm and encouraging place for women who love their families, want to grow in faith, and are trying to find God in the middle of ordinary life.

What is the Honey Hive?

The Honey Hive is the Honey & Hymns membership community. Members receive additional resources, weekly Sunday Gospel materials for kids, exclusive articles, and early access to new posts and projects.

What's the difference between the monthly and yearly Honey Hive memberships?

Both memberships include the same Honey Hive benefits. The yearly membership is $50 per year, which is 17% less than paying $5 each month for a full year.

What is the Beekeeper's Circle?

The Beekeeper's Circle is for Honey & Hymns founding members who want to help support the brand as it grows. Beekeepers receive all Honey Hive benefits, special recognition on the Honey & Hymns website, Substack, and magazine, plus a first look at major Honey & Hymns projects.

Do I have to be a member to read Honey & Hymns?

Nope! Many Honey & Hymns articles and resources are available completely free. If you'd like to receive new articles, weekly bulletins, and other Honey & Hymns content right in your inbox, you can even subscribe for free.

Where can I find the free resources?

You can find all of the currently available free resources in the Free Downloads section of the Honey & Hymns website. I’m always adding new resources as the Hive grows, so be sure to check back from time to time!

I purchased a product and I'm having trouble with it. What should I do?

I'm happy to help. For any product issues, download problems, or questions about something you've purchased, please email:

honeyandhymns.support@gmail.com

Can I suggest an article or topic?

Absolutely. If there's something you'd love to see Honey & Hymns write about, send it my way through the contact form. I love hearing what other moms are thinking about, struggling with, and learning.

How can I contact Honey & Hymns?

If you don't find the answer you're looking for here, simply fill out the contact form on this page and I'll get back to you as soon as I can.

Thank you for taking the time to stop by. Every message is a little gift. 🐝
