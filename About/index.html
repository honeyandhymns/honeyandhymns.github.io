A Little About Me

I'm Just a Mom Figuring It Out Too

Hi, I'm Annie.

I'm a Catholic wife, mom, homemaker, and homeschooler trying to build a beautiful family life in the middle of all the ordinary, messy, wonderful things that come with it.

Honey & Hymns grew out of that ordinary life. It began with a desire to create a place where women could find encouragement without feeling like they had to have everything figured out first.

Here you'll find stories about homeschooling, homemaking, motherhood, faith, marriage, books, routines, and the little things that make up family life.

Some days are peaceful. Some days are absolutely not. But I believe there is something beautiful about learning to find God in both.

🐝

The Heart Behind Honey & Hymns

Faith isn't something we only practice when life is tidy. It's woven into the meals, the lessons, the laundry, the prayers, the hard days, and the little moments of joy.
